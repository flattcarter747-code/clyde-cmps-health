## What is organ donation?

**Organ donation** means giving an organ to someone whose own organ has stopped working. Most donations happen after a person dies. Organs that can be donated include the **heart, lungs, liver, kidneys, pancreas,** and **intestines**. People can also donate **eyes (corneas)** and **tissue**, like skin, bone, and heart valves.

Some people donate while they're alive. A **living donor** can give one kidney or part of their liver to someone who needs it.

## Why it matters

Members of our own team have family who have been on the transplant list, so this topic is personal for us. Waiting for an organ is hard on the whole family. Every person who signs up gives someone else a better chance at more time with the people they love.

Signing up is free and only takes a few minutes. Most importantly, **tell your family** so they know your wishes.
