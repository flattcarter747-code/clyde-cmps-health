## What is blood sugar?

When you eat, your body breaks food down into sugar (called **glucose**) for energy. A hormone called **insulin** works like a key that lets sugar move from your blood into your cells.

- With **type 2 diabetes**, your body doesn't use insulin well, so sugar builds up in your blood.
- With **type 1 diabetes**, the body makes little or no insulin. It is usually found in kids and young adults and can't be prevented.
- **Prediabetes** means your blood sugar is higher than normal, but not high enough to be diabetes yet. It's a warning light.

## Why it matters

Over time, high blood sugar damages blood vessels and nerves. That can lead to **heart disease, stroke, kidney disease, vision loss,** and **nerve damage in the feet**.

## Sugar and your heart

Many people at our booth asked how sugar affects the heart. Eating and drinking a lot of **added sugar** (the kind in soda, candy, and sweet tea) is linked to weight gain, high blood pressure, and higher triglycerides, a type of fat in your blood. All of these make heart disease more likely. Cutting back on sugary drinks is one of the easiest ways to protect both your blood sugar and your heart.
