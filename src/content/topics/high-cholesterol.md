## What is cholesterol?

**Cholesterol** is a waxy, fat-like substance in your blood. Your body needs some to build cells. Your liver makes all you need, and you also get some from foods like meat, eggs, and dairy.

There are two main kinds:

- **LDL ("bad") cholesterol** can stick to the walls of your arteries and build up into **plaque**.
- **HDL ("good") cholesterol** carries extra cholesterol back to your liver to get rid of it.

## Why it matters

When plaque builds up, your arteries get narrow and stiff. That makes it harder for blood to flow and can lead to a **heart attack** or **stroke**.

Because high cholesterol doesn't cause symptoms, many people don't know they have it. A simple blood test, healthy habits, and medicine (if your doctor recommends it) can keep your heart healthy for years to come.
