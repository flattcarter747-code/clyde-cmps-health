## What is blood donation?

When you **donate blood**, a trained worker uses a clean, new needle to collect about a pint of blood from your arm. Your body replaces the fluid within a day or two.

Donated blood is tested, separated into parts (**red cells, plasma,** and **platelets**), and sent to hospitals, where it helps patients who need it.

## Why it matters

Blood is needed every day for surgeries, cancer treatment, accidents, and childbirth. It only lasts for a short time on the shelf, so blood centers always need new donors.

Hospitals in Abilene depend on donors from our area, including Clyde. Giving blood is one of the easiest ways to help someone in our own community, maybe even someone you know.
