## What is CPR?

**CPR** stands for **cardiopulmonary resuscitation**. When someone's heart suddenly stops beating (called **cardiac arrest**), CPR keeps blood and oxygen moving to the brain until help arrives.

**Hands-Only CPR** is CPR without mouth-to-mouth breaths. It's recommended for anyone who sees a teen or adult suddenly collapse. You don't need a certificate to do it, and Texas law protects people who help in an emergency in good faith.

## Why it matters

Out here, help can take a few extra minutes to arrive. In cardiac arrest, every minute counts. A bystander who starts CPR right away can be the difference between life and death.

Several of us on the team have family members who have had heart attacks. Learning CPR is one of the most useful things anyone, of any age, can do for their family and community.
