## What is blood pressure?

Every time your heart beats, it pushes blood through tubes called arteries. **Blood pressure** is how hard that blood pushes against the walls of your arteries.

A reading has two numbers, like **120/80**:

- The **top number** is the pressure when your heart beats.
- The **bottom number** is the pressure when your heart rests between beats.

**High blood pressure** (also called *hypertension*) means the push is too strong, too much of the time.

## Why it matters

High blood pressure makes your heart work harder and slowly damages your arteries. Over time, that can lead to a **heart attack, stroke, kidney disease,** or **vision problems**.

The tricky part is that it usually doesn't hurt. Lots of people in our community feel fine and have no idea their numbers are high. The good news is that blood pressure is easy to check, and small changes really do help.
