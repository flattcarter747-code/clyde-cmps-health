## What is mental health?

**Mental health** is how we think, feel, and handle everyday life. It affects how we deal with stress, get along with others, and make choices. Everyone has mental health, just like everyone has physical health.

Everyone has hard days. A **mental health condition**, like depression or anxiety, is different. It lasts longer, feels heavier, and gets in the way of daily life.

## Why it matters

Your mind and body are connected. Long-term stress and depression can affect your sleep, your heart, and your blood pressure. Health worries, like caring for a sick family member, can also weigh on your mind.

In a small town, it can feel hard to talk about how you're doing. But asking for help is a sign of strength, not weakness. **Treatment works**, and you don't have to figure it out alone.
