## What is obesity?

**Obesity** means having more body fat than is healthy for you. Doctors usually start with a number called **BMI** (body mass index), which compares your weight to your height. For adults, a BMI of 30 or higher is called obesity.

Obesity is not about how someone looks, and it's not a character flaw. Our genes, sleep, stress, medicines, what foods are easy to get, and how much time we have to move all play a part.

## Why it matters

Extra weight makes your heart, joints, and blood sugar work harder. Over time, that raises the risk of **type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure, heart disease, stroke,** and **some cancers**.

Here's the encouraging part: you don't have to change everything at once. Small, steady changes, like drinking more water and walking after dinner, can make a real difference in how you feel and in your numbers.
